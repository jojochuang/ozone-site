# Release Notes for Ozone 1.4.1

Apache Ozone 1.4.1 was released on March 31st, 2025. This version brings numerous enhancements and bug fixes.

## Key Highlights

This maintenance release includes important bug fixes and improvements to enhance the stability and performance of Apache Ozone 1.4.

For detailed information about all the features, improvements, and resolved issues in this release, please refer to the [Apache Ozone JIRA project](https://issues.apache.org/jira/browse/HDDS).

## Getting Started

- Download Apache Ozone 1.4.1: [https://ozone.apache.org/downloads/](https://ozone.apache.org/downloads/)
- Documentation: [https://ozone.apache.org/docs/1.4.1/](https://ozone.apache.org/docs/1.4.1/)

Thank you for your continued support, and we look forward to your feedback on this release!
